# Feature specification: measured outcomes
Feature dir: specs/vk/d1d7-sexy-measured-ou/
Status: specified

## Summary
Make the resume stand on its own after referral by foregrounding documented business results and explaining technical difficulty. Keep the strongest Netflix-relevant API and partner-support stories visible.

## User stories
- A hiring reader can see results, scope, personal contribution and the mechanism behind them without needing the referrer to explain.
- David can defend each changed claim and identify which missing metrics need collecting.

## Functional requirements
- FR-1: Lead the summary and selected achievement bullets with supported results; retain role dates and ownership.
- FR-2: Preserve reported, approximate, personal-project, requeue and postponed-work qualifications. Do not invent revenue, FTE savings or reliability deltas.
- FR-3: Explain the relevant technical obstacle or mechanism in each rewritten achievement.
- FR-4: Document source mapping and missing baseline/after measurements. Keep public files sanitized.
- FR-5: Apply voice review, increment the resume version and preserve published rendering and URLs.

## Out of scope
New telemetry investigations, applications or outreach, employment timeline changes, changes to hiring, new professional media experience or language-proficiency claims.

## Acceptance criteria
- Changed claims have a before/after factual mapping and no unsupported increase in scope.
- Summary and bullets show supported outcomes and retain technical specificity.
- Voice validation, render and repository checks pass; independent review has no significant findings.
- Knowledge records measurement gaps and collection methods; PR is merged after checks.

## Open questions
- Resolved from evidence policy: retain an honest operational result or scope where no baseline exists. Record the missing measurement and collection method separately. Additional candidate figures are optional and require their basis before use. No blocking questions remain.
