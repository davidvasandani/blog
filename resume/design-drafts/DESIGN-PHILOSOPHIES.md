# Resume design philosophies

The conceptual thread is systems made understandable: the spacing, boundaries, and repeated marks quietly reflect infrastructure and operational clarity. The resume text is the essential content, preserved in full; the skill’s minimal-text approach applies to ornament and labels. All designs use US Letter pages.

## 01. Swiss Precision

An asymmetric grid turns hierarchy into a clear visual rhythm. A cobalt square establishes a single point of emphasis. Space carries the structure before a word is read. Each boundary should feel intentional rather than ornamental.

The palette pairs #214AC7 with #FFFFFF. Color is used as a navigation cue, while dark body text maintains contrast. Large flat areas remain quiet and free of texture.

InstrumentSans Bold establishes the primary voice; InstrumentSans Regular supports sustained reading. Scale changes are measured, and repeated intervals create a consistent cadence.

The composition calls for meticulous craftsmanship: aligned baselines, controlled line lengths, and enough separation for each unit to stand on its own. The final refinement should show painstaking attention to spacing, not additional decoration.
